SEWA Hackathon · Project Idea

LaborLink

Fair, transparent work for India’s daily-wage skilled workers. LaborLink matches households with nearby plumbers, electricians, masons and carpenters. It shows a fair price up front, tracks the worker on the way, and builds every worker a record of their earnings.

The problem

Skilled daily-wage workers mostly find work at labour chowks, through middlemen and by word of mouth. This informal market is inefficient for both sides.

Employers

“What’s wrong, who do I call, and what should it cost?”

  • It’s hard to describe a technical problem precisely.
  • There’s no reference for a fair price, so they overpay or haggle.
  • They can’t easily find a trusted worker nearby.
  • They don’t know when the worker will actually arrive.
Workers

“Will I get work today, and will I be paid fairly?”

  • Income is unpredictable and depends on middlemen.
  • They haggle with no reference point and are often underpaid.
  • There’s no verifiable record of the work they’ve done.
  • Without that record, they stay out of formal credit and welfare schemes.

Our solution

LaborLink is a mobile-first app with two sides, one for employers and one for workers. It makes every job clear, fairly priced and recorded, from the first photo to the final payment.

Describe

Photo + voice intake

The employer snaps a photo and speaks the problem in English, Hindi or Hinglish. No forms, and it works for users with low literacy.

Understand

Structured work order

The request becomes a clear job card with the trade, problem, likely tools and materials, duration, complexity, and questions to ask.

Price

FairRate

An explainable price range for labour plus travel. Every rate, multiplier and assumption is shown.

Match

Nearby workers & fair offers

Matching workers in range see the job. Each one accepts the suggested pay or counter-offers within fair limits.

Track

Live trip tracking

After booking, the employer sees the worker on a live map: “Arriving in 8 min”, a journey bar and the road route.

Record

Kamai Passbook

Photo-verified, employer-confirmed jobs and earnings build a digital work history for each worker.

Who it’s for

Households

Families who need a repair done today and want a fair price and a reliable worker.

Small businesses & contractors

Shops, offices and site supervisors hiring skilled labour job by job.

Skilled daily-wage workers

Plumbers, electricians, masons and carpenters looking for steady nearby work and proof of their earnings.

Partner organisations

Groups such as SEWA that support informal workers could later use passbook data, with consent, to connect them to schemes and credit.

The user journey

  1. Describe the problemThe employer adds a photo, then speaks or types what’s wrong and picks the urgency.
  2. Get a work order and a fair priceLaborLink shows the structured job card and a FairRate range, with “How we worked it out”.
  3. Workers make offersNearby matching workers accept the suggested pay or counter-offer within the allowed range.
  4. Book a workerThe employer compares offers by amount, distance and experience, and books one with a tap.
  5. Watch them arriveThe worker starts the trip. The employer sees a moving arrow on the map, the ETA and “At your door”.
  6. Do the workThe worker swipes to start the job, then uploads before and after photos when it’s done.
  7. Confirm and payThe employer reviews the photos and confirms, or reports a problem. Payment is simulated in the prototype.
  8. Grow the passbookThe job and its earnings appear in the worker’s Kamai Passbook.

Features

Employer app
  • Onboarding in English or Hindi, with location permission
  • Photo upload and voice-to-text job description
  • Work order and FairRate price card
  • Live offers list with one-tap booking
  • Full-screen live tracking with ETA, journey progress and worker details
  • “Worker on the way” banner on the home screen
  • Completion review of before/after photos, then confirm or dispute
  • Simulated payment and job history
Worker app
  • Profile with trades, experience, area, service radius and availability
  • Find work: nearby matching jobs with the suggested pay
  • Accept or counter-offer
  • Trip screen with live GPS sharing (or a demo trip), Google Maps navigation and swipe-to-start
  • Before/after photo evidence
  • Kamai Passbook: jobs, earnings and payment status
Platform
  • REST API with OpenAPI docs
  • Rule-based work-order engine that runs offline, with an optional Claude vision model
  • Configurable rate card and offer limits
  • Job lifecycle with strict state transitions
  • Location-based matching (haversine distance within the service radius)
  • 259 automated backend tests

What makes it different

PrincipleWhat it means in LaborLink
Explainable pricingEvery estimate lists its rate source, multipliers, assumptions and warnings. Rates are labelled as illustrative, never as official or legal wages.
Fair bargainingCounter-offers must stay within a floor and ceiling around the fair range, which protects workers from undercutting and employers from gouging.
Honest uncertaintyMissing details and follow-up questions are shown rather than guessed. Observations from photos are never presented as confirmed facts.
Built for low literacyVoice input, icons, Hindi labels, large touch targets and a simple step-by-step flow.
Trust through visibilityLive trip tracking, photo evidence and employer confirmation before any payment.
Worker-owned recordThe Kamai Passbook turns informal gigs into a verifiable work and earnings history.

How it works

A Next.js web app, styled as a phone, talks to a FastAPI backend over a REST API. Data is stored in SQLite.

Frontend

Next.js 14, React and TypeScript. Leaflet draws the OpenStreetMap map, and road routes come from OSRM. Voice uses the browser’s speech recognition, and location uses the browser’s GPS.

Backend

FastAPI, Pydantic and SQLAlchemy. Services cover work orders, pricing, matching, offers, booking, tracking, completion, settlement and the passbook.

Data

A SQLite file for the prototype. Uploaded photos are stored on disk, and the rate card is a JSON file.

Expected impact

For workers

Steadier nearby work, fairer pay, less dependence on middlemen, and a record that can support access to credit and social security.

For employers

Faster hiring, prices they can trust, and less anxiety about who is coming and when.

For the ecosystem

Anonymised data on skills demand and fair rates that could inform skilling and policy.
