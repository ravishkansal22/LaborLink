OCR (reading text from images)

Optical character recognition reads printed or handwritten text in photos. In LaborLink it would save typing and help build trust.

Use case

Worker documents

Read the name and number from a skill certificate (ITI, Skill India), e-Shram card or trade licence during onboarding. Store only what’s needed, with consent.

Use case

Appliance labels

Read the model number and rating plate from a photo of a pump, geyser, MCB or fan, so the worker knows which spare part to bring.

Use case

Material bills

Read the total from a shop receipt for materials the worker bought, and add it to the job’s settlement and the passbook.

Use case

Handwritten notes

Turn a contractor’s handwritten job list or measurements into text.

Hardware, data & people

Hardware

  • Developer laptops: 8 GB RAM recommended for running Next.js and the backend together
  • At least one Android phone with GPS and a microphone for field testing
  • A pilot server: a small cloud instance (1–2 vCPU, 2–4 GB RAM) is enough at the start

Data

  • A verified rate card per city and trade, to replace the illustrative rates
  • Lists of localities with coordinates for each pilot city
  • Sample voice notes and photos in Hindi and regional languages, to test speech-to-text and OCR

People & partners

  • A field partner such as SEWA to onboard workers and gather feedback
  • Legal review of terms, privacy policy and payment flows
  • Support for disputes and help requests

Rules for keys:
• Keep them in backend/.env locally (git-ignored) and in the cloud secrets manager in production. Never put them in code, screenshots or chat.
• Only variables starting with NEXT_PUBLIC_ reach the browser, so never give a secret that prefix.
• Use separate keys for development and production, set usage limits, and rotate a key straight away if it leaks.

Compliance & privacy to plan for

Location data

Share it only while a job is booked or in progress, with clear consent. Keep only the latest position (as the prototype already does), and use routing that doesn’t send locations to third parties.

Personal data

Follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: consent, a stated purpose, data minimisation and the right to delete.

Payments & KYC

Real money movement and identity checks must go through RBI-regulated partners. They are out of scope for the prototype.

Voice, photos & documents

Voice notes, OCR’d IDs and photos are sensitive. Get consent, keep only the fields you need, store them in private buckets, and set retention limits. Don’t store full Aadhaar numbers.

Fair pay

Check FairRate ranges against the applicable state minimum wages before showing them as anything other than illustrative.
